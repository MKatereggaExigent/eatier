Standards

Quality you can trust

We hold ourselves and our platform to the highest standards so you can use Itiyum with confidence.

Data Privacy

Your data is yours. We encrypt everything, never sell user information, and give you full control over your privacy settings.

Verified Listings

Every business on Itiyum is manually verified to ensure authenticity. Fake or duplicate listings are removed promptly.

Fair Reviews

Our review system is designed to surface genuine experiences. We actively monitor for spam, bias, and manipulation.

Quality Content

From menus to photos, we encourage high-quality content that helps users make informed decisions.

Limitations

What we don't do

We believe in being honest about where our platform can and cannot help, so you can set the right expectations.

No Direct Ordering

We facilitate connections and bookings, but we do not process payments or handle food delivery logistics directly.

No Food Delivery

Itiyum is a discovery and management platform. We don't operate a delivery fleet or manage last-mile logistics.

No Legal or Health Advice

We provide tools and data, but we do not offer legal, regulatory, or health and safety advice to businesses.

No Guaranteed Results

While our tools are designed to help, we cannot guarantee specific business outcomes, sales, or customer volumes.

Not a Replacement

Itiyum complements your existing operations but is not a substitute for your own website, POS system, or in-person service.
